Work with us.

We build three things for companies, on an open network you can inspect: a digital twin of how your company works, the open infrastructure under it with someone to call when it matters, and engineering results that come with a certificate anyone can re-check. Everything on this page links to something that already runs.

What we offer · 3

  • Company digital twins

    A twin of your company that works for the people in it

    Sites, lines, staff dispatch, contracts and renewals in one live model, with an owner view, a manager view per site and a view for each person of their own record. It shows how the work flows, not how each person is watched.

    • Nothing passive about people: no keystrokes, screenshots, webcams, microphones or emotion scoring.
    • Each person sees their own record first, and who looked at it.
    • Owners get site, team and shift numbers; groups under five show as “fewer than 5”.
    • No scores of people. A missing record becomes a question to the person, never a deduction.
    • People can export their own work record and take it with them.
    Proof: a three-station line twin that signs every event; your browser checks 320 of 320 signatures and recomputes the numbers. A working owner/manager/worker demo is shown on a call.

  • Open infrastructure + support

    The open layer, and help running it

    ce-net is an open protocol and runtime where every service is a cell with its own key. Capabilities can only narrow as they are handed on, and anyone can verify them offline. The protocol is Apache-2.0; the starter packs are MIT.

    • Turn a program you already run into a network service with a manifest and a health check.
    • Keys that do exactly one thing, expire, or work only on your own devices.
    • Signed receipts for every call, checkable with one Python file.
    We help you set it up on your machines, wrap your programs as cells and keep them running. Your data stays on your hardware.

  • Engineering verification

    Engineering results you can re-check

    A result is worth what you can check. Our finite element runs come with a signed certificate that binds the solver source, the input and the output, and any browser can re-run the solver and get the same hash.

    • Four load cases solved on an ARM server and an x86 desktop: identical output bytes, and the same hash again in the browser.
    • FEM tip deflection within 0.6 % of beam theory on every case.
    • 440 of 440 single-field edits to a certificate rejected.
    Datum, our engineering proof language, re-checks claims like “the bracket does not yield” with exact arithmetic and returns proven, refuted or undetermined, with the inequality that decided it. Shown on a call; not public yet.

How a first project goes

  1. A 30-minute call

    You tell us what you run and where work gets held up. We show the demo twin and say which of its numbers are simulated.

  2. One area, scoped

    We pick one site, line or station and write down what the twin covers and what it leaves out, before any work starts.

  3. It runs on your machine

    The twin runs on your hardware. A person approves every outside action, and every step is logged back to its source.

  4. You keep it

    Open protocol, your data, your copy. If you stop, you keep the record and the open parts.
